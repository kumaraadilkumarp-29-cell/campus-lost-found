How the register works

Four steps, one verified handover.

Report it

Describe the item, the zone and the date. Add a photo if you have one. Two minutes, no forms to print.

Match it

The moment you post, the portal looks for the opposite report in the same category and alerts both sides.

Claim it

Explain privately why the item is yours. Only the person who filed the report can read your proof.

Return it

Approve the claim, meet at a public campus spot, and the listing closes as recovered.

Hand it back safely

The register never publishes phone numbers, room numbers or student IDs. Every handover happens on campus, in daylight, with a witness if you can.

  • 01 Meet at the security desk, library entrance or canteen — never a hostel room.
  • 02 Ask for one detail that is not in the public description.
  • 03 Never pay a “finder’s fee” to recover your own property.
  • 04 Report anything suspicious to the campus security office.
